Online stores now expose WebMCP tools that AI agents can call. Agents can search your catalog, manage the shopper's cart, and go to checkout on the shopper's behalf, all in the tab they're looking at. The tools work through WebMCP , a proposed web standard that lets a page register tools with the browser.
