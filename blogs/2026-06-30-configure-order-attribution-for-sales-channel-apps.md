Starting in API version 2026-07 , sales channel apps can use order attribution definitions to identify the source that created an order. Order attribution definitions are useful when your sales channel app needs attribution that is more specific than the app or channel itself. For example, you can attribute orders to a marketplace, region, account, or surface.
