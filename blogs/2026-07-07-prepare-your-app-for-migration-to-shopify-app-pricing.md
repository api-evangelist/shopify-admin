Developers using the Billing API can now begin preparing their apps to move to Shopify App Pricing. The first part of our migration tool lets you generate, review, edit, and test Shopify App Pricing plans before moving any existing merchant subscriptions. This gives you time to confirm that your pricing configuration is ready while your app continues using its current billing setup.
