Today we're sharing the newest wave of improvements to the Dev Dashboard. You told us, in the community forums and in support conversations, that common work like managing collaborator access or understanding app health took too much juggling. This release brings that work into one home for your apps and the stores you work in: store management, collaborator access, and app health—one place to see how your business is doing and act on whatever needs attention, across all your apps and stores.
