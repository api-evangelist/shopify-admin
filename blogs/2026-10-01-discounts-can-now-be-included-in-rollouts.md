Update your app to account for discount Rollouts Summary Admin GraphQL API version 2026-10 adds a read-only rollouts connection across Discount types. Apps that read discounts should use Discount.rollouts alongside existing discount fields to account for Rollout-driven schedules and buyer reach. What's changing Merchants can include discounts in Rollouts to coordinate launches, test discounts with a share of buyers, or run temporary events alongside other store changes.
