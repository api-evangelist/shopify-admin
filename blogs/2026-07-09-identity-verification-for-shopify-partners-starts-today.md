Trust is foundational to the Shopify Partner ecosystem. Partners take actions across Shopify that can directly affect merchants, and merchants need confidence when granting access to their stores. We’re introducing identity verification for partners to help protect merchants, reduce abuse, and make it harder for bad actors to operate, while creating a better experience for legitimate partners.
