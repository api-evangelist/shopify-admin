With today's release of AI Toolkit , all existing shopify- skills are consolidated into a single shopify skill. If you use AI Toolkit skills with agents or npx skills , you might need to update your environment. What changed Shopify skills content in AI Toolkit was previously distributed across many skills, such as shopify-dev-onboarding , shopify-admin , and shopify-functions .
