Apps that bill with the Billing API can now move their existing subscriptions to Shopify App Pricing by using the migration tool in the Partner Dashboard or new commands in Shopify CLI. Subscriptions move at the start of their next billing cycle, and merchants don’t need to re-approve charges. What changed You can now migrate existing Billing API subscriptions to Shopify App Pricing instead of creating new plans from scratch.
